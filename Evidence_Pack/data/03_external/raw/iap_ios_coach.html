  • Pretty Tracker, Pretty App, Meh Data
    12/11/2018
    Tea-Flow

    I really wanted a nice looking fitness tracker so I could wear it all the time. My Fitbit started looking pretty sad after a year or so and it definitely looks like an athletic tracker. I always wore my Bella Beat as a bracelet, because I knew swapping it out as a clip or necklace would be more annoying. But the bracelet is kind of bulky. I was mostly worried about whether it would work as well as my fit bit and I think the answer is no. Sleep tracking wasn’t great, steps seemed low compared to my phone that doesn’t move more than I do. I was hoping for an app that allowed better integration with other services but that seemed limited. I’d personally much rather import the data over to the health app where I can track everything. And I’ve always been frustrated with the lack of options for integrating the vibrating alarm with more than just an alarm that’s not super east to adjust.I’m really intrigued by the new watch, because I’ve always wanted a normal watch that also functions like a fitness tracker, but at the end of the day I just keep thinking I’d be better off prioritizing function over form.

    04/03/2019
    DK9717

    I have the original leaf Nature from 2015. I only wore it a couple months back then and quickly tired of it I guess. Been wearing it every day of 2019. I find my device to be glitchy at times. This app is ok but I really think it could be better!

Oftentimes I go without my phone so I like that it’s still counting. I would appreciate a bigger focus on sleep. More pages of sleep data, more viewing options for comparisons. Also, more exploration around poor sleep data: the app counts my restless nights as sleep and so “reduces” my stress susceptibility graph thing. One more practical thing: the leaf comes off of the chain fairly easily. I’ve noticed at least five times in the short time I’ve had it. I regularly sleep on my stomach, and in the night I have gotten up and it falls to the floor! So, something for the design team: as a necklace, make it less likely to fall off!
