      How NC° Birth Control works

      The AI-enhanced algorithm behind the app confirms each user’s daily fertility status by analyzing temperature data, menstrual data & optional LH tests. To prevent pregnancy, users agree to abstain or use protection on fertile days.

      Is it right for my patient?

      NC° Birth Control may be a good option for those seeking a non-hormonal, non-invasive birth control method.

      NC° Birth Control is suitable for those who:

      • Are 18 years or older
      • Agree to use protection or abstain on fertile days
      • Cannot tolerate hormonal contraception
      • Currently use a period tracker or less effective method

      NC° Birth Control is certified to be used as a contraceptive in the US, Canada, EU, UK, Australia, South Korea,Singapore and Brazil.

      Prescribe NC° Birth Control via eRx

      NC° Birth Control is a non-hormonal contraceptive method that’s FDA-cleared. It may be a option for those who:

      • Are 18 years or older
      • Agree to use protection or abstain on fertile days
      • Cannot tolerate hormonal contraception
      • Currently use a period tracker or less effective method
      • Are looking to prevent pregnancy now, with a view to plan pregnancy in the future

      NC° Birth Control is just as effective for users with irregular cycles, but they may be assigned more fertile days, which may reduce satisfaction.

      Note that patients cannot use NC° Birth Control at the same time as hormonal contraception.

      NC° Birth Control is the first and only contraceptive app to be FDA-cleared and CE marked. As such, the Natural Cycles app is a regulated medical device.

      The AI-enhanced algorithm uses body temperature and other fertility indicators to confirm each user’s daily fertility status. Peer-reviewed research found that the algorithm finds fertile days more accurately than calendar-based methods (specifically, the Rhythm Method and Standard Days Method), and that it is less likely to incorrectly assign a non-fertile day. Read more about this research here.

      Yes! Effectiveness remains 98% Perfect Use and 93% typical use even in those with irregular cycles. They just may experience more red days. 

      Data security is a top priority for Natural Cycles and NC° Birth Control is designed to protect user data. Natural Cycles Data Protection Officer and Team use advanced security protocols, including pseudonymization and encryption, as part of our comprehensive privacy program.

      Users who need an extra layer of identity protection have the option to Go Anonymous. In this optional mode, personal identifying information is separated from fertility data. That means that no one — not even Natural Cycles — can link a user to their data.

      Because Natural Cycles charges a subscription fee, it doesn’t need to earn money by selling users’ data like some “free” apps. As a regulated medical device, Natural Cycles is required to comply with the FDA’s cybersecurity requirements. And as a Swedish company, the Natural Cycles app is designed to comply with European GDPR.
