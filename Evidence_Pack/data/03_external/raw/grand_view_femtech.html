FemTech Market Summary

The global femtech market size was valued at USD 45.6 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow from USD 52.9 billion in 2026 to USD 145.5 billion in 2033, at a CAGR of 15.5% from 2026 to 2033. North America dominated the market, accounting for the largest share of 45.7% in 2025. The growth of the femtech industry is driven by rising awareness of women's health, the adoption of digital healthcare solutions, and the growing demand for technologies for fertility, pregnancy, menstrual, and menopause management.

Market Dynamics

Driver: Expansion of AI-Powered Women’s Health Platforms

AI-powered women’s health platforms are accelerating the shift toward personalized and preventive care. These solutions support menstrual tracking, fertility planning, pregnancy monitoring, menopause management, and early risk assessment using real-time health data. AI-enabled platforms also improve access to care through virtual consultations, predictive analytics, and tailored health recommendations.

For instance, in June 2025, NITI Aayog’s Frontier Tech Hub recognized Mind & Mom, a Chennai-based femtech company, for its AI-driven maternal healthcare platform. The platform has supported over 200,000 women through menstrual cycle tracking, fertility planning, IVF guidance, pregnancy monitoring, and personalized maternal health recommendations. Similarly, at CES 2025, IdentifyHer launched Peri, an AI-enabled wearable for perimenopause support, demonstrating the growing use of AI in underserved areas of women’s health.

Restraint: Data Privacy and Regulatory Concerns Limiting User Trust

Data privacy concerns are restraining the femtech market’s growth, as platforms collect sensitive information such as menstrual cycles, fertility patterns, pregnancy status, reproductive history, and hormonal health data. Concerns about the misuse or sharing of this data can erode user trust and slow adoption of fertility-tracking apps, menstrual-health platforms, and digital reproductive-health solutions.

Regulatory scrutiny is also increasing compliance costs for femtech companies. For instance, in 2023, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission took action against Easy Healthcare Corporation, developer of the Premom fertility tracking app, over allegations of sharing sensitive reproductive health data with third parties without adequate consent. Such cases highlight the need for stronger data protection, consent management, and privacy compliance, which can increase operational burden and limit scalability, especially for early-stage startups.

Opportunity: Expansion of Employer-Sponsored Women’s Health Benefits

The growing adoption of employer-sponsored women’s health benefits is creating opportunities for femtech companies. Employers are increasingly offering fertility, maternity, postpartum, menopause, and reproductive health support to improve employee wellness and retention, driving demand for digital platforms that provide virtual consultations, care navigation, and personalized health programs.

For instance, in 2024, Maven Clinic raised USD 125 million in Series F funding, reaching a valuation of USD 1.7 billion, supported by rising demand from employers and health plans. The company serves more than 2,000 customers across 175 countries through fertility, pregnancy, parenting, and menopause care solutions. Similarly, companies such as Carrot Fertility are supporting employer-sponsored fertility benefits, including fertility preservation, assisted reproduction, adoption, and surrogacy navigation. This shift is expected to support demand for subscription-based and enterprise femtech platforms.

The femtech industry operates under stringent regulatory frameworks due to the clinical nature of many products, including medical devices, diagnostic software, and digital therapeutics. Regulatory approvals from agencies such as the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), the European Medicines Agency (EMA), and the UK Medicines and Healthcare Products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) are essential before commercialization. In 2024, the FDA continued expanding oversight of AI-enabled Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), requiring robust clinical validation, cybersecurity, and lifecycle monitoring for AI-based diagnostic tools. These evolving regulatory requirements increase development costs but also improve product quality, safety, and market confidence.

Companies are rapidly expanding beyond fertility and pregnancy solutions into menopause care, pelvic health, breast health, cardiovascular risk assessment, mental health, hormonal disorders, and preventive care. In May 2025, Maven Clinic expanded its integrated digital care platform by introducing enhanced menopause and midlife health services alongside fertility and maternity offerings for employers and health plans. Similarly, several femtech companies launched AI-enabled screening tools, at-home diagnostics, and virtual care services to address previously underserved areas of women's health, significantly broadening the industry's service portfolio.

Integration of AI in the FemTech Industry

AI is transforming the FemTech industry by enabling more predictive, personalized, and data-driven women’s healthcare solutions. AI-powered platforms are increasingly being used for menstrual health management, fertility tracking, pregnancy monitoring, menopause care, and early disease detection, improving diagnostic accuracy and clinical decision-making.

For instance, in June 2026, Xella Health launched an AI-powered precision health platform focused on XX-chromosome biology to improve diagnostic accuracy and address gaps in women’s health research. Similarly, in June 2025, Flo Health adopted the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform to strengthen AI-driven cycle prediction and personalized health recommendations.

These developments highlight the growing role of AI in improving personalization, early intervention, user engagement, and data-driven care delivery across the industry.

Type Insights

The devices segment dominated the femtech market with a revenue share of 33.4% in 2025 and is expected to grow at the fastest CAGR of 15.70% during the forecast period. Growth is driven by the rising adoption of wearable devices for menstrual health, fertility tracking, pregnancy monitoring, and menopause management. Advancements in biosensors, AI, wireless connectivity, and real-time monitoring are further increasing demand for connected femtech devices.

For instance, in 2025, Movano Health advanced the commercialization of its Evie Ring. This women-focused smart ring tracks menstrual cycles, sleep, heart rate, blood oxygen levels, stress, and other health metrics, reflecting the growing demand for personalized, continuous health-monitoring solutions.

The software segment accounted for the second-largest market share in 2025 and is expected to grow significantly during the forecast period. Growth is supported by rising smartphone penetration, digital healthcare adoption, and increasing demand for AI-powered women’s health apps used for menstrual tracking, fertility planning, pregnancy support, menopause care, and virtual consultations.

For instance, in May 2025, HeraMED partnered with Teladoc Health to integrate its digital pregnancy monitoring platform with Teladoc’s virtual care ecosystem, supporting the growing use of software-enabled femtech solutions for remote pregnancy monitoring and personalized maternal care.

Application Insights

The pregnancy and nursing care segment dominated the femtech industry with a revenue share of 17.7% in 2025. Growth is driven by the increasing adoption of digital pregnancy monitoring, virtual maternity care, remote patient monitoring, and connected maternal health solutions. Companies are developing AI-enabled pregnancy apps, fetal monitoring devices, lactation support platforms, and personalized maternity care solutions to improve prenatal and postpartum care.

For instance, in May 2025, HeraMED partnered with Teladoc Health to integrate its remote pregnancy monitoring platform with Teladoc’s virtual care services. The partnership supports real-time fetal and maternal health monitoring, strengthening the adoption of digital prenatal care solutions and driving segment growth.

The menstrual health segment is expected to register the fastest CAGR of 16.3% during the forecast period. Growth is supported by rising awareness of menstrual health, increasing prevalence of PCOS and endometriosis, and growing demand for digital cycle-tracking solutions. Advances in AI, wearables, and personalized analytics are encouraging the adoption of menstrual health apps and connected devices.

For instance, in 2025, Flo Health expanded its AI-powered menstrual and reproductive health platform with enhanced cycle predictions, symptom analysis, and educational support. This reflects the growing demand for intelligent menstrual health solutions and supports the segment’s long-term growth.

End-use Insights

The direct-to-consumer segment dominated the femtech market with a revenue share of 30.7% in 2025 and is expected to register the fastest growth during the forecast period. Growth is driven by the convenience of online access, rising smartphone penetration, and increasing preference for personalized women’s health products and services. Consumers are increasingly using DTC platforms for fertility monitors, menstrual health apps, pregnancy care devices, menopause solutions, and wellness products.

For instance, in January 2025, Perelel Health expanded its personalized vitamin subscription platform with formulations for fertility, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and menopause. This reflects the growing demand for convenient, subscription-based, and personalized women’s healthcare solutions, supporting DTC segment growth.

Research Methodology

The FemTech market figures in this report are based on a proven research process that combines executive interviews with secondary research from proprietary databases, company filings, and recognized regulatory and institutional sources. Market size is built through value-chain sizing-reconciling supply-side and demand-side estimates-and triangulated with bottom-up and top-down approaches. Methodology Overview

The FemTech market size was estimated using a bottom-up approach, beginning with the identification of the potential target female population across key application areas. Market penetration rates for various FemTech products and services were then derived through secondary research and primary interviews. Average selling prices and service costs were analyzed across segments and regions. The target population, penetration rates, and pricing assumptions were integrated to estimate segment-level revenues, which were subsequently aggregated to derive the global FemTech market size and forecast.
